01 · Profile

Research across imaging, algorithms, and measurement.

A compact overview of my academic interests and working approach.

About

I work on computational imaging problems in which the observation model, reconstruction algorithm, and measurement process must remain physically consistent. My current interests span hyperspectral and multispectral image fusion, spectral reconstruction, deep unfolding, computational optics, and infrared temperature measurement.

A recurring goal in my work is to connect algorithmic performance with interpretable physical models and experimentally verifiable quantities, rather than treating sensing and reconstruction as separate stages.

02 · Research

Research directions

Three closely related themes connect optical sensing, inverse reconstruction, and traceable measurement.

01

Hyperspectral & Computational Imaging

High-resolution hyperspectral reconstruction, HSI–MSI fusion, spectral reconstruction, semi-blind inverse imaging, and computational imaging systems.

02

Physics-guided Learning

Deep unfolding, model-based neural networks, degradation estimation, inverse problems, and learning methods that retain explicit links to the image formation model.

03

Infrared Metrology & Thermometry

SI-traceable infrared temperature measurement, blackbody calibration, radiometric transfer, and large-area temperature-field reconstruction.
